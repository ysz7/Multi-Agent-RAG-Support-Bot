Northwind Cloud Troubleshooting Notes

Login loop after SSO change
If members are bounced back to the login page after an SSO configuration change,
the identity provider metadata is stale. Re-upload the metadata XML and ask
affected members to clear their session cookie. Sessions issued before the
change remain valid for one hour.

Missing webhook deliveries
Check the deliveries tab before contacting support. Deliveries that never left
the queue appear as "pending" for at most five minutes; anything older than that
is a delivery failure and carries the HTTP status returned by the endpoint.

Slow exports
Exports larger than 1 GB are split into multiple files of 1 GB each and emailed
as download links. The links expire after 48 hours.

Duplicate records after import
A repeated import creates duplicates only when the external ID column is absent.
Re-run the import with the external ID column mapped, then use the merge tool.

Two-factor lockout
A member locked out of two-factor authentication is reset by an account owner
from the members page. Support cannot reset two-factor authentication on behalf
of a customer.
