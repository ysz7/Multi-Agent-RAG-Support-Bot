# Northwind Cloud Support Handbook

Northwind Cloud is a fictional SaaS product. This corpus exists so the golden
dataset has stable, unambiguous ground truth.

## Support hours

Standard support is staffed Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 20:00 UTC. Weekend cover
is on-call only and handles Severity 1 incidents. Public holidays follow the UTC
calendar published on the status page.

## Response targets

First response targets are measured from the moment a ticket is created:

- Severity 1 (service down for all users): 30 minutes, 24/7.
- Severity 2 (major feature unusable): 4 business hours.
- Severity 3 (degraded or inconvenient): 1 business day.
- Severity 4 (question or feature request): 3 business days.

Response targets apply to the first human reply, not to resolution.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued within 30 days of the original purchase. After 30 days, only
account credit is available, and credit expires 12 months after it is issued.
Refunds are returned to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business
days to appear.

### Partial refunds

A partial refund applies when a subscription is cancelled mid-cycle on an annual
plan. The unused whole months are refunded; the current month is not.

## Escalation

A customer may request escalation at any time. Escalation moves the ticket to a
senior engineer and notifies the duty manager. Severity 1 tickets escalate
automatically after 60 minutes without a first response.

## Account deletion

Deleting an account is irreversible and is performed only on written request
from an account owner. The deletion runs after a 7-day grace period, during
which the request can be cancelled by replying to the confirmation email.
