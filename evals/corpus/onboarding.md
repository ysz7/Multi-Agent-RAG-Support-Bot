# Onboarding

## Creating a workspace

A workspace is created by the first person to sign up with a company email
domain. That person becomes the account owner. A workspace can have at most two
account owners.

## Roles

There are four roles:

- Owner: billing, security settings, and deletion.
- Admin: members, integrations, and project settings.
- Member: normal day-to-day use.
- Viewer: read-only access, does not consume a paid seat.

Viewers are free on every plan. Role changes take effect immediately.

## Inviting members

Invitations are sent by email and expire after 7 days. An expired invitation can
be resent from the members page. Invitations to a domain not on the allow-list
require owner approval.

## Data import

Data can be imported from CSV or from a supported competitor export. An import
of up to 100 MB runs immediately; larger imports are queued and run overnight.
Imports are atomic: a failed import leaves the workspace unchanged.

## Trials

A trial lasts 14 days, requires no credit card, and includes Team plan features.
At the end of a trial, a workspace without a payment method becomes read-only
for 30 days before it is scheduled for deletion.
