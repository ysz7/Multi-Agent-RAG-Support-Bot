# Billing

## Plans

Northwind Cloud has three plans:

- Starter: 19 USD per user per month, up to 10 users, community support.
- Team: 49 USD per user per month, up to 200 users, standard support.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, unlimited users, 24/7 support and a named contact.

Annual billing is discounted by 20% compared with paying monthly.

## Invoices

Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing period and are due 14 days
after issue. Invoices are available as PDF from Settings, Billing, Invoices.
A failed payment is retried three times over 10 days before the account is
suspended.

## Proration

Adding seats mid-cycle is prorated to the day and charged on the next invoice.
Removing seats does not generate a refund; the seat count drops at the start of
the next billing period.

## Payment methods

Accepted payment methods are credit card, SEPA direct debit, and bank transfer.
Bank transfer is available on annual Enterprise contracts only. Payment by
cheque is not accepted.

## Taxes

Prices exclude VAT. A valid EU VAT number entered in billing settings removes
VAT from future invoices under the reverse-charge rule. Past invoices are not
reissued.
