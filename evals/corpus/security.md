# Security and Data Protection

## Data retention

Customer data is retained for 24 months after the last account activity, then
deleted automatically. Backups are retained for 35 days. Audit logs are retained
for 12 months on Team and 24 months on Enterprise.

## Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Encryption
keys are rotated annually.

## Authentication

Single sign-on is available on the Enterprise plan and supports SAML 2.0 and
OIDC. Two-factor authentication is available on every plan and can be enforced
for all members by an account owner. SCIM provisioning is Enterprise only.

## Subprocessors

The current subprocessors are the hosting provider, the email delivery provider,
and the error-tracking provider. Customers are notified 30 days before a new
subprocessor is added.

## Incident response

A confirmed security incident is reported to affected customers within 72 hours
of confirmation. The post-incident report is published within 14 days.

## Compliance

Northwind Cloud is SOC 2 Type II certified and reports are available under NDA.
There is no ISO 27001 certification at this time.
